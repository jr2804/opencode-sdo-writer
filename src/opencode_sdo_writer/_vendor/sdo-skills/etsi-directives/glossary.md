# Glossary

**3SI Advocate** — person nominated by the Annex III Organisations to support the joint interest of their representatives and experts within ETSI; ex-officio non-voting Board member (Ch 7)

**Additional Member** — a Full or Associate member in the same Academic/Corporate/Public Group as a Primary Member which contributes financially to the ETSI budget (Ch 3)

**Administrative Fee** — the flat contribution paid by an Additional Member in a Corporate or Public Group (Ch 3)

**Affiliate** — entity linked to a member by >50 % ownership or control; references to a MEMBER include its AFFILIATES (Ch 4)

**Annex III Organisations** — the European stakeholder organisations defined in Annex III of amended Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 (Ch 3)

**Association of undertakings** — ETSI's character under Article 101 TFEU, alongside its role as a recognized standards body (Ch 5)

**Basic Co-operation Agreement** — the CEN/CENELEC/ETSI agreement containing the five cooperation modes and the Joint Presidents' Group (TWP Annex P) (Ch 11)

**Board** — body acting for the General Assembly between GA meetings within delegated powers; elected + ex-officio non-voting members (Ch 7)

**Class of Contribution** — the class a Full or Associate member falls into per its GDP, ECRT, category or type, determining its annual contribution and voting weight (Ch 3)

**Collective Letter** — the Director-General's communication to all ETSI members about an accept/reject decision on a Standardisation Request (Ch 9)

**Consensus** — general agreement characterized by the absence of sustained opposition to substantial issues by any important part of the concerned interest, reached by a process involving all views (Ch 3)

**Contribution schemes** — the four ETSI schemes: GDP, ECRT, Flat and Special Scale (Ch 3)

**Coordination Group (CG)** — a coordination structure initiated by ETSI (TWP Clause 4) or by external bodies (Clause 5); the OCG is explicitly *not* one (Ch 11)

**Counsellors** — the European Commission and the EFTA secretariat; attend the GA and participate in Board/NSOG/NSBG/Technical Group work, with no vote (Ch 3)

**Courtesy title** — recognition granted by the General Assembly by consensus to a former Chair/Vice-Chair, Board member or Secretariat member (Ch 10)

**Directives** — the compilation of 17 governance instruments (v53, 1 September 2026); each instrument is separately adopted and separately numbered (Ch 1)

**Director-General (DG)** — legal representative and chief executive authority, ex-officio non-voting member of GA and Board, appointed by the GA for 5 years extendable once by up to 3 (Ch 3)

**Docbox** — the ETSI document repository (docbox.etsi.org), one of the surfaces covered by the Information Policy (Ch 12)

**ECRT (Electronics Communications Related Turnover)** — the worldwide turnover of a member's products and services in the fields of Statutes Arts 2–3 (Ch 3)

**editHelp!** — the ETSI website hosting deliverable skeletons and drafting guidance referenced by the EDR (Ch 13)

**EDR (ETSI Drafting Rules)** — Director-General-adopted rules for the structure and drafting of deliverables, complementing the TWP (Ch 13)

**ENAP (EN Approval Process)** — the process by which eligible National Standards Organizations adopt European Standards not derived from an SReq (Ch 3)

**EP (ETSI Project)** — market-sector Technical Body with a well-defined result, limited duration, and responsibility for its Work Items (Ch 11)

**EPP (ETSI Partnership Project)** — Technical Body for cooperation with external partners; approves its own deliverables, may have its own working procedures and legal status, and may manage its own finances (Ch 11)

**ESSENTIAL (IPR)** — an IPR whose use is unavoidable, on **technical (not commercial)** grounds, to implement a standard (Ch 4)

**ETSI Work Programme (EWP)** — the record of all Work Items, milestones and status flags across Technical Groups (Ch 11)

**Fellow (ETSI Fellowship)** — merit award decided by the Award Committee (GA Chair + Vice-Chairs + Board Chair + DG), requiring four external nominators; may be posthumous (Ch 10)

**Finance Committee (FC)** — GA-appointed advisory committee on budgetary and financing management; consultative unless duly committed (Ch 8, Ch 9)

**FRAND** — fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory licensing; the ETSI IPR regime's default, with no preference for royalty-free (Ch 4)

**GDP Contribution Scheme** — the scheme applying exclusively to Full/Associate members in the Administrations category, keyed to national GDP classes (Ch 3)

**GoSR (Group on Standardisation Requests)** — the SReq coordination forum under the DG's leadership; it does not accept SReqs (Ch 9)

**Group (Academic / Corporate / Public)** — the ETSI construct governing contribution and voting: academic entities, company groups, and the Administration + OGBs of one country (Ch 3)

**Head of National Delegation** — the person appointed by a National Delegation, notified to the DG, who casts the delegation's weighted national vote (Ch 3)

**Historical status** — a deliverable status in which the document is kept unchanged in the EWP until a withdrawal decision is taken (Ch 11)

**hEN (Harmonised Standard)** — a European Standard adopted on an EC request, giving presumption of conformity; withdrawal is covered by RoP Art. 21.6 (Ch 3, Ch 13)

**Interested Parties (SReq context)** — the group comprising the Board, OCG, NSBG, NSOG, Annex III Organisations and Counsellors, plus Ad Hoc members (Ch 3, Ch 9)

**Intentional Delay** — deliberate withholding of an IPR disclosure significantly beyond normal timeliness; where proven, a Policy breach sanctionable by the GA (Ch 4)

**ISG (Industry Specification Group)** — a fast technical lane established on the initiative of ≥ 4 members, producing GR/GS only, and barred from EC/EFTA-mandated work (Ch 11)

**Legal master** — the single authoritative version of an ETSI deliverable (Ch 13)

**MAP (Membership Approval Process)** — the 60-day all-members vote by which ESs and EGs are adopted (Ch 11)

**Mode 5 (Integrated relation)** — the deepest CEN/CENELEC cooperation mode, producing Joint Technical Bodies with a designated leading ESO (Ch 11)

**Modal verbs** — the EDR's compliance vocabulary: shall (requirement), should (recommendation), may (permission), can (possibility), will (inevitability), is (fact) (Ch 13)

**National Delegation** — the group of Full-member representatives registered in the same country; votes via its Head of National Delegation (Ch 3)

**National Standardisation Body (NSB)** — a National Standards Organization listed by the EC in the OJEU; the NSB Group (NSBG) is the SReq decision-making body (Ch 3)

**National Standards Organization (NSO)** — an organization performing at national level the Standstill, Public Enquiry, national-position, transposition and withdrawal functions; the NSO Group (NSOG) comprises CEPT-country NSOs that signed the ETSI-NSO Agreement (Ch 3)

**OCG (Operational Coordination Group)** — the cross-Technical-Body deconfliction forum reporting to the Board, chaired by a Board Vice-Chair (Ch 9)

**OJEU** — the Official Journal of the European Union, where harmonised standard citations are published (Ch 13)

**PAS (Publicly Available Specification)** — a submission route producing TS or TR only, with the Rapporteur designated by the PAS Submitter (Ch 11)

**Patent Family** — all documents sharing at least one priority, including the priority document(s) (Ch 4)

**Primary Member** — the member of an Academic/Corporate/Public Group holding the Group's voting weight (Ch 3)

**Public Enquiry** — the mandatory national enquiry conducted by NSOG members before a draft EN is submitted for adoption (Ch 3)

**Rapporteur** — the single named individual responsible for a Work Item, its technical contact point and the updater of maturity milestones (Ch 11)

**Reciprocity** — the condition on which an IPR holder's FRAND licence undertaking may be made dependent (Ch 4)

**Reserved seats** — Board seats the GA may allocate to ensure representation of specific membership categories or types (e.g. Users, SMEs) (Ch 7)

**Retired Friend of ETSI** — status applied for in writing and approved by the Director-General (Ch 10)

**SDG (Software Development Group)** — a software/open-source development activity hosted by ETSI; it must not develop ETSI Deliverables (Ch 11)

**Secretariat** — the body providing logistical support, headed by the Director-General (Ch 3)

**Specially Convened Meeting (SCM)** — the GA meeting type required to amend the Statutes or Rules of Procedure; ≥ 6 weeks' notice (Ch 1, Ch 3)

**Special Scale Fee** — the contribution payable by an NSO or NSB in certain circumstances to take part in voting activities (Ch 3)

**SReq (Standardisation Request)** — an EC request to draw up and adopt European Standards or European standardization deliverables supporting EU legislation and policies (Ch 3)

**SRdAP** — the Standardisation-Request deliverables Approval Process, with NSBG exclusive competence (Ch 3)

**Standstill** — the obligation not to undertake national standardization activity that could prejudice preparation of an EN; applies to individual Work Items, starting 60 days after WI adoption (Ch 3)

**Successors-in-interest** — transferees bound by an IPR holder's FRAND undertaking as an encumbrance (Policy 6.1bis) (Ch 4)

**TB (Technical Body)** — an EP, TC or EPP; the primary decision-making centre for matters in its ToR (Ch 11)

**TC (Technical Committee)** — a technical-area Technical Body with no predefined term limits, handling tasks common to multiple projects (Ch 11)

**Technical Group** — a Technical Body or comparable group (including ISGs/SDGs) with a Chair, membership and decision-making procedures (Ch 11)

**Transposition table** — the mandatory element ending the Foreword of every EN, provided by the Secretariat (Ch 13)

**TWP (Technical Working Procedures)** — the Board-adopted procedures for the Technical Organization, Special Committees, ISGs and SDGs (Ch 11)

**Unnumbered clause** — front-matter clause that must not be renumbered: IPR, Foreword, Modal verbs terminology, Executive summary (Ch 13)

**Void annex** — a retired-in-place annex retained so that subsequent numbering stays stable (e.g. RoP Annexes 4–5; TWP Annexes F, I, O) (Ch 1)

**Weighted Individual Voting** — weighted voting by Full members (RoP 11.2.2) or by Full and Associate members (11.2.3, the default) (Ch 3)

**Weighted National Voting** — voting by National Delegations with Annex 3 country weightings; used for ENs, amendments, dissolution and RoP disputes (Ch 3)

**WI (Work Item)** — the description of a standardization task, supported by ≥ 4 Full and/or Associate members and adopted by its Technical Group (Ch 11)

**WISR** — a Work Item identified as a constituent part of a Standardisation Request (Ch 11)

**Working Group (WG)** — a subgroup established by a Technical Body, which decides its rules but retains responsibility (Ch 11)
