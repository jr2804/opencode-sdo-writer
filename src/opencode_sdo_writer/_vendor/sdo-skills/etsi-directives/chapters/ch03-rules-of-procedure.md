# Chapter 3: Rules of Procedure

**Version adopted by the Special Meeting of the General Assembly #87a (27 August 2026)** — 22 Articles + Annexes 1, 2, 3, 6 (IPR Policy, see Ch 4), 7 (Board rules, see Ch 7). The operational constitution: it decides *who votes, how, and with what weight*, and it runs the standards-approval machinery.

## Core Idea

Everything the Statutes leave open is fixed here: membership conditions, the General Assembly's 38 enumerated powers, the three distinct voting procedures and their trigger cases, quorum and proxy rules, and the two parallel standards-approval processes (ENAP for ETSI-originated standards, SRdAP for Standardisation-Request work). The **71 % approval threshold** and the **weighted-vote architecture** are the load-bearing mechanics.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **The three voting procedures (Art. 11.2)** — the central decision framework. Which one applies is determined by the *subject matter*, not by choice:
  - **11.2.1 Weighted National Voting** (National Delegations, weights per Annex 3). Applies to: ETSI dissolution; disputes arising from applying the RoP; **adoption and withdrawal of European Standards** (except SReq-derived ones); **amendments to the Statutes and RoP including annexes**; allocation of weightings to new National Delegations.
    - A National Delegation's vote may be cast only if a Full member of that country in the **Administrations** category financially contributes to the ETSI budget. Cast by the **Head of National Delegation**, after taking the views of all members of the delegation into account.
  - **11.2.2 Weighted Individual Voting by Full members** (weights per Annex 2). Applies to: admonition/termination/rights of Full members; documents intended for **regulatory use by the EU** (excluding SReq matters); standardization policies to meet EU needs; Work Programme priorities applying **exclusively inside the EU**.
  - **11.2.3 Weighted Individual Voting by Full and Associate members** — **the default**: applies in *all cases other than* those in 11.2.1 and 11.2.2.
  - **Common rules**: approval requires **≥ 71 % of the votes cast** unless the RoP provide otherwise; abstentions and late/non-votes **do not count as votes cast**; where a Group holds a stake, only **one member per Academic/Corporate/Public Group** votes (the last vote cast counts); a weighted vote is a **single entity and cannot be split** (except under Annex 7).
- **Decision by Consensus first (Art. 11.1.1)**: in all decisions members shall endeavour to reach **Consensus**, defined in Annex 1 Point 5 as general agreement characterized by the absence of sustained opposition to substantial issues by any important part of the concerned interest, via a process seeking to consider all views. Minority rights are to be protected.
- **Secret ballot (Art. 11.5–11.6)**: the norm, except when the Directives require comments on negative votes or the Chair decides otherwise (in consultation with the DG, and only before the vote starts). Never waivable for: expulsion of a member by the GA; election/dismissal of the GA Chair and Vice-Chairs, FC Chairs, Technical Group, SDG, Coordination Group, NSOG and NSBG Chairs; election/dismissal of Board members and Chair; appointment/dismissal/extension of the DG.
- **Election procedure (Art. 11.4)**: for GA Chair/Vice-Chairs, DG, and Chair of the Board — first ballot requires 71 %; if not met, a second ballot; with two candidates the higher vote wins; with more than two and still no 71 %, a **third and final ballot between the top two**.
- **Quorum and proxies (Art. 12)**: quorum for any of the three procedures ≥ **40 % of the total weighted votes of Full members**; Weighted National Voting additionally requires ≥ **50 % of the total weighted votes of National Delegations**. Correspondence votes under Arts 13.5/21.5 need ≥ 50 % of votes cast including explicit abstentions; other correspondence votes have no minimum. Proxy votes require written notice to the DG **at least 17 hours** before the meeting, and **no member may cast more than three (3) proxy votes in a single decision**.
- **Amendment majorities (Art. 19)**: proposals go to a **Specially Convened Meeting** with **≥ 6 weeks' notice** including an agenda and the proposed amendments. Approval requires **≥ 75 % of total weighted votes for the Statutes** and **≥ 71 % of total weighted votes for the Rules of Procedure including annexes**.
  - The Statutes' Article 18 and the RoP's Article 19 must be read together: 18 sets the instrument, 19 sets the figure.
- **ENAP — the EN Approval Process (Art. 13)**: the route for European Standards not requested by the EC.
  - A draft EN may be proposed by **at least four (4) Full and/or Associate members**.
  - **Participation restricted** to the Annex III Organisations and the NSO Group members; the NSOG performs Standstill, Public Enquiry, establishment of the national position for the adoption/withdrawal vote, national transposition, and national withdrawal under the **ETSI-NSO Agreement**.
  - Where a country has several NSOs, any may vote but **only one vote per country** counts; votes may be changed before closure.
  - Voting right conditioned on an ETSI member in the Administrations category contributing under the **GDP Contribution Scheme**.
  - Adoption/withdrawal by Weighted National Voting (Art. 11.2.1), administered by the DG, normally **online** (or at a meeting announced ≥ 30 days ahead).
  - On a successful vote, the EN is adopted in the EU/EFTA countries **and** in non-EU/EFTA countries that voted in favour.
- **Standstill (Art. 13.3)**: the obligation not to undertake national standardization activity that could prejudice preparation of an EN.
  - Applies to an **individual Work Item** with a precise scope and target date — **not** to areas or programmes of work.
  - Starts **60 calendar days** after adoption of the new WI (or at the start of drafting, whichever is later); imposed and released by the GA; remains in force until released or the EN is withdrawn; any member or NSO may request a review.
  - **Not infringed** by (a) an NSO publishing, within 3 months of Standstill start, a national standard it has already adopted; (b) publishing, after notifying the GA, a national standard adopting without change a published ITU Recommendation or an ISO/IEC standard — in both cases the NSO must commit to aligning with any eventual EN.
  - Derogations require a **written request to the GA**, which must decide **within 6 months** of receipt.
- **Public Enquiry (Art. 13.4)**: a mandatory step before a draft EN is submitted for adoption, carried out by NSOG members and administered by the DG; comments from NSOG members and Annex III Organisations must be given due consideration.
- **ES/EG — ETSI Standards and ETSI Guides (Art. 14)**: drafted by TCs, EPs, Special Committees or EPPs, then adopted by **Weighted Individual Voting** (11.2.2/11.2.3/11.3) and published by the DG. All Full and Associate members may vote; if not adopted, the vote distribution among Full and Associate members is analysed. Withdrawal follows the same procedure.
- **SReqs and the SRdAP (Arts 20–21)**: the parallel route for EC-requested work — a structurally different process with different voters.
  - An **SReq** is an EC request to draw up and adopt European Standards or European standardization deliverables supporting EU legislation and policies.
  - On receipt, the DG notifies all **Interested Parties** (Board, OCG, NSBG, and others per Annex 1 Point 5).
  - **Examination** runs in parallel with the NSB voting procedure; the DG organizes it and communicates conclusions in time for NSBs to cast an informed vote.
  - **NSB vote on acceptance**: a **25 calendar-day** Weighted National Voting procedure organized by the DG; results disseminated immediately.
  - **Acceptance notification**: within **30 calendar days** of receiving the SReq, the DG notifies the EC, EFTA, and Interested Parties of the NSBG's accept/reject decision.
  - **WISR**: adoption/rejection of the Work Items identified in the SReq is voted by NSBG members.
  - **SRdAP** (Art. 21): participation by Annex III Organisations and the NSOG; **the NSBG holds the exclusive competence** for national positions on (i) acceptance/rejection of the SReq, (ii) approval/rejection of new WISRs, and (iii) adoption/withdrawal of the deliverables — while the NSOG keeps Standstill, Public Enquiry, transposition and withdrawal duties.
  - Adoption/withdrawal by Weighted National Voting (Art. 21.5); withdrawal also covers **Harmonised Standards**.
- **Escalation (Art. 22)**: three tiers of appeal, mirroring the Statutes' appeal rights.
  - **GA**: any member may appeal against any decision (including the decision-making process) taken by the Board or at Technical Organization level — initiated by contacting the GA Chair and/or submitting a written contribution to an upcoming GA meeting, with a clear description of the issue.
  - **Board**: any member may appeal against any decision taken at Technical Organization level, by Special Committees under Board responsibility, and by ETSI-initiated Coordination Groups.
  - **Director-General**: any member delegate (including elected officials) or staff member may report perceived misconduct; the DG investigates and reports the outcome to the reporter and, where applicable, to the leadership of the body concerned.
- **Membership mechanics (Art. 1)**: resignation by notice to the DG — notice given **before 30 September** takes effect at the end of the current financial year, otherwise membership (and the contribution) continues. Expulsion by the **DG** for non-payment (after final demand); from **1 June until payment**, voting rights and participation in Technical/Coordination/SDGs are suspended. Expulsion for other substantial breach is by the **GA** by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, the member entitled to furnish an explanation, by secret-ballot vote. **Counsellors** (EC/EFTA) may attend the GA and participate in Board, NSOG, NSBG and Technical Group work, but in SDGs neither contribute technically nor supply software unless they accept the relevant licence conditions.
- **General Assembly machinery (Art. 3)**: the GA determines general policy, management and strategy; comprises Full members (grouped in National Delegations where applicable), Associate and Observer members, and Counsellors; National Delegations appoint a Head of National Delegation notified to the DG; Guests may attend by invitation of the Chair. Its **38 powers** (3.4.1–3.4.38) span elections, adoption of the Statutes/RoP/Financial Regulations, membership and Counsellor questions, DG appointment, contributions and budget, the annual report, auditor appointment, dissolution, standardization policy, external agreements, EPP decisions, approval of draft standards (except SReq deliverables), Standstill matters, unresolved technical matters, RoP disputes, disputes between Technical Groups, appeals, the ETSI Work Programme and its priorities and resources, the structure of the Technical Organization, creation/cessation of TCs/EPs/ISGs/Coordination Groups/SDGs and their ToRs, appointment of TC/EP Chairs, STF approval and funding, Special Committees, approval of the Technical Working Procedures, and election/delegation/supervision of the Board.
  - **Documentation**: numbered `ETSI/GA(YY)NNN_xxx`; classified **Decision / Discussion / Information** (a decision may be taken on a Discussion or Information document if there are no objections); decision-document cut-off is **10 calendar days** before the meeting; late submissions are marked with a cross but do not prevent a decision — though **any member may request deferral**.
  - **Electronic meetings** must allow participant identification and contribution, oral expression of views, and live broadcast of debates.
- **Officers (Arts 4, 8)**: GA Chair and Vice-Chairs must be representatives of **Full members**; **2-year terms, one further consecutive term**; nominations in writing to the DG **30 days** before the meeting, proposed by ≥ 2 Full or Associate members (or 1 Full + 1 Associate) from **two different Groups and two different countries**; criteria include experience, equitable geographical distribution, and not sharing nationality/category/Group; preference for a **Users-category Vice-Chair** if the Chair is not from Users. Loss of a supporting Full member triggers a **3-month transition** (Guest status, no chairing, no written contributions) and a **confirmation vote needing ≥ 71 %** of weighted individual votes. The **DG** serves a **5-year term, extendable once by up to 3 years**; the post is publicly advertised; a selection committee is appointed about two GA meetings ahead; the shortlist should hold 3–6 candidates (minimum 2).
- **Finance (Art. 10)**: financial year **1 January – 31 December**; provisional contributions are due in full on **1 January** and may be invoiced in instalments; payment after **30 April** incurs a **5 % penalty**; unpaid by **1 March** triggers a further demand before the final demand; outstanding contributions are annexed to the DG's progress report. Budgetary excess up to **150 k€** goes entirely to the special reserve fund; above that, **10 % of the excess above the threshold**. Applicant dues scale by poll: **1st poll 100 %, 2nd 75 %, 3rd 50 %, 4th 25 %**; missing a payment deadline defers the application (still liable); missing twice cancels it; an objection sends the application to the next Ordinary GA.
- **Languages (Art. 17)**: official GA languages are **English, French and German**; the **working language of every ETSI group (including Board and OCG) is English**; deliverables are adopted and published in English, though an adopted EN may be translated and published equally authentically.
- **Disputes (Art. 18)**: member-to-member disputes first by internal conciliation with the help of other members, then **French law**. Member-vs-Institute claims must first exhaust all appeal procedures; the member bears the Institute's reasonable defence **Costs** unless it prevails; French law governs, excluding conflict-of-law provisions.

## Key Concepts

- **Consensus**: absence of sustained opposition, not unanimity (Annex 1 Point 5).
- **National Delegation**: Full-member representatives registered in the same country; its vote cast by the **Head of National Delegation**.
- **Annex III Organisations**: European stakeholder organisations defined in Annex III of amended Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 — represented by a **3SI Advocate**.
- **NSOG / NSBG**: the NSO Group (National Standards Organizations from CEPT countries having signed the ETSI-NSO Agreement) and the NSB Group (its subset of bodies listed by the EC in the OJEU); the NSBG is the decision-making body for SReq-related national positions.
- **Interested Parties (SReqs)**: the group comprising the Board, OCG, NSBG and others — the consultation set for SReq handling.
- **Standstill**: the national-activity freeze on an individual EN-bound Work Item.
- **Harmonised Standard**: a European Standard adopted on an EC request, giving presumption of conformity (withdrawal covered by Art. 21.6).
- **Weighted vote as an indivisible entity**: cannot be split across proposals.
- **Class of Contribution**: the GDP/ECRT/category-based class determining an annual contribution and its associated voting weight.

## Mental Models

- "**Subject matter picks the voter**" — ask what is being decided, and Art. 11.2 names the procedure; there is no discretion.
- "**Default is Full + Associate, weighted individually**" — if it is not dissolution, an EN, an RoP dispute, an amendment, a new weighting, a Full-member rights question, an EU-regulatory document, an EU standardization policy, or an EU-only Work Programme priority, it is 11.2.3.
- "**71 % is the number**" — nearly every approval threshold in the RoP is 71 % of votes cast, with abstentions excluded; the Statutes amendment is the outlier at 75 %.
- "**Two lanes of standardization**" — ENAP (ETSI-originated, NSOG + Annex III Organisations, Weighted National Voting) vs SRdAP (EC-requested, NSBG competence, 25-day and 30-day clocks).
- "**Consensus is a process, not a tally**" — the absence of sustained opposition by an important part, reached by involving all views.
- "**Groups vote through one voice**" — a Corporate/Public/Academic Group casts a single weighted vote, the last cast counting.

## Anti-patterns

- **Counting abstentions as "no" votes**: abstentions and non-votes are excluded from the denominator ("votes cast") — a proposal can pass on a minority of the full membership subject to quorum.
- **Splitting a weighted vote across proposals**: prohibited — a vote must be cast or withheld in its entirety (Annex 7 has the sole specific exception).
- **Using an Ordinary GA to amend the RoP**: amendments need a Specially Convened Meeting with ≥ 6 weeks' notice and ≥ 71 % of total weighted votes (75 % for the Statutes).
- **Assuming the NSOG and NSBG are interchangeable**: the NSBG holds the *exclusive* competence for SReq acceptance, WISR approval and SReq-deliverable adoption/withdrawal; the NSOG keeps Standstill, Public Enquiry, transposition and withdrawal.
- **Treating Standstill as applying to a programme of work**: it attaches only to an individual Work Item with a precise scope and target date.
- **Forgetting the 17-hour proxy deadline or the 3-proxy cap**: both are hard procedural limits at the meeting.
- **Letting a Group cast multiple votes**: only one member organization per Group is considered; the last vote cast prevails.

## Worked Example

**Choosing the right procedure for four decisions.** Apply Art. 11.2 by subject matter:

1. *Adopt a European Standard proposed by six ETSI members* → an EN, not SReq-derived → **Weighted National Voting** (11.2.1), via ENAP, after a Public Enquiry and with Standstill having started 60 days after WI adoption. Needs ≥ 71 % of votes cast, ≥ 40 % Full-member quorum **plus** ≥ 50 % National-Delegation quorum.
2. *Adopt an ETSI Standard (ES)* → not in the 11.2.1 or 11.2.2 lists → **Weighted Individual Voting by Full and Associate members** (11.2.3), per Art. 14.
3. *Set a standardization policy intended to meet EU needs* → expressly in the 11.2.2 list → **Weighted Individual Voting by Full members only**; Associate members do not vote.
4. *Amend the Rules of Procedure* → **Weighted National Voting** (11.2.1), but in a **Specially Convened Meeting** with ≥ 6 weeks' notice, and the threshold rises to **≥ 71 % of total weighted votes** (Art. 19) rather than 71 % of votes cast.

- Takeaway: the same 71 % figure means different denominators — "votes cast" for Art. 11.3 decisions, "total weighted votes" for amendments — and the amendment case changes the meeting type as well as the majority.

## Key Takeaways

1. The RoP fixes the decision architecture: three weighted procedures selected by subject matter, with 11.2.3 as the default.
2. 71 % of *votes cast* is the standard threshold; abstentions are excluded; amendments instead need 71 % (RoP) or 75 % (Statutes) of *total weighted votes*.
3. Two parallel standards lanes — ENAP (own proposal, NSOG-led) and SRdAP (EC SReq, NSBG-led, 25-day/30-day clocks).
4. Standstill binds an individual EN-bound Work Item for 60 days after adoption; derogations are GA decisions with a 6-month deadline.
5. Quorum, proxy (17 hours, max 3) and Group single-voice rules constrain the arithmetic before any vote is counted.
