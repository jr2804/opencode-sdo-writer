# Chapter 11: Technical Working Procedures (TWP)

**Version adopted by Board #158 (25 June 2026)** — the largest instrument in the Directives. It complements **RoP Articles 6, 7, 11, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, 20 and 21** and applies to the Technical Organization, Special Committees, ISGs and SDGs.

## Core Idea

The TWP is where standards actually get made: it defines the **three types of Technical Body**, how Chairs are appointed and dismissed, how **Work Items** (WI and WISR) are created and stopped, how technical bodies **decide** — and, crucially, the two-speed approval architecture: documents that need a **membership vote** (EN via ENAP, ES/EG via MAP) versus documents the **Technical Body simply adopts** (TS, TR).

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Scope and the retention of final authority (Introduction)**: the TWP complements the named RoP Articles and covers joint technical work with CEN/CENELEC under the **Basic Co-operation Agreement** (Annex P). Three authority reservations matter: the **GA keeps final authority over functions delegated to the Board** (RoP Art. 3), and — separately — over functions delegated to the Director-General for **ISGs** (RoP 8.3.9) and **SDGs** (RoP 8.3.14).
- **The three Technical Bodies (TWP 1.1)** — the choice of type is a governance decision, not a label:
  - A TB provides the forum for technical discussion, prepares work programmes and deliverables, and is **the primary decision-making centre for all matters within its Terms of Reference**; it may establish Working Groups (deciding their rules) but **retains responsibility**; unresolvable intra-organizational coordination issues may be raised to the **OCG**.
  - **ETSI Project (EP)** — a *market-sector* activity with a **well-defined result**, **limited duration**, defined resources, its own project/programme management and timed deliverables; responsible for creating, adopting and stopping WIs; **subcontracts work to TCs where competence requires**; reports to the GA on request.
  - **Technical Committee (TC)** — organized around a *technical area* defined in its ToR, with **no predefined term limits**; handles technology-oriented tasks **common to more than one EP/EPP** and unsuitable for the project format; performs work subcontracted from EPs and EPPs.
  - **ETSI Partnership Project (EPP)** — established when cooperation with **external bodies** cannot be accommodated in an EP or TC; market-sector based with **limited duration**; participation by Full and Associate members **plus significant external participation**; it may have **its own working procedures, policies and Secretariat support defined in an agreement or contract with the Partners that may vary from the TWP**; it is **responsible for approving its own deliverables including validation**; it has a **defined legal status for liability and copyright** and **may manage its own financial affairs**; it may decide the nature of its deliverables and hence their approval/publication route.
  - **Choosing the body type** is guided by **Annex D** (requirements for the establishment of a new body) and **Annex E** (choice of ETSI deliverable type).
- **Creation and cessation (TWP 1.2)**: the **Board approves creation/cessation of a TC or EP**; the **General Assembly approves an EPP**. A TB about to cease must first: prepare maintenance recommendations with the Secretariat; ensure its WIs are **published or stopped**; and propose a Technical Group to maintain its deliverables. On formal acceptance by the receiving group and Board approval, responsibility transfers; if none accepts or none is identified, the DG acts; where no suitable body exists the Board is informed and the deliverables **remain unchanged and publicly available**. Joint Technical Bodies with CEN/CENELEC are **mode 5** of the Basic Co-operation Agreement.
- **Chairs of Technical Groups (TWP 1.3)**:
  - **Appointment principles**: the Chair **shall be a delegate of a Full or Associate member organization and a member of the Technical Group**; appointed **by the Technical Group** for a **two-year term**; up to **three consecutive terms**; beyond three only if **no other candidate applied fourteen calendar days** before the appointment procedure; after the maximum, a new series only after a **gap of at least one term**; the term limits bind **both the person and the member organization or Group**; and **the appointment is confirmed by the Board**.
  - **Procedure**: a call for candidature is launched; the candidate list is circulated beforehand; **a single candidate may be appointed by Consensus**; with several, the Clause 1.7 decision-making applies; if the TG cannot decide, the matter is **put on hold and referred to the Board**.
  - **Exit routes**: **dismissal by the Board** for breach of responsibilities (a Vice-Chair stands in, and a dismissed Chair may no longer apply); **resignation** (Vice-Chair stands in, Board informed); **unavailability** through long-term sickness or incapacity (Vice-Chair stands in until the next TG meeting); **loss of supporting organization** (three-month transition with a Vice-Chair as interim and the former Chair attending as Guest; if no new Full/Associate member is found the TG assumes resignation, and **the term served still counts as a full term**).
  - **Vice-Chairs and Working Group Chairs** are appointed for two years using the Clause 1.7 procedures, renewable after each period.
  - **Responsibilities**: overall management of the Work Programme and efficient running of the TG including WGs; ensuring activities comply with the ETSI Directives and procedures; reporting to the Board and GA on request; and **maintaining strict impartiality, acting in the interest of ETSI and its members**.
- **Work Items (TWP 1.6.1)** — the unit of standardization work:
  - A **Work Item is the description of a standardization task**; a Technical Group's Work Programme consists of its WIs. **Only one (1) Technical Group is responsible for each WI**, though assistance from others is expected. A new high-level WI that does not fit one TG's scope is **submitted to the OCG** for coordination and allocation. **Where work will produce more than one deliverable, a separate WI is created for each.** A WI may legitimately have **no expected deliverable**, clearly indicated as such.
  - **A single individual is named Rapporteur per WI**, acting as prime contact point on technical matters and progress.
  - **Creation routes**: a **WI** proposed by members, or a **WISR** identified as a constituent part of an SReq.
  - **WI parameters** (Annex K form): title; intended ETSI deliverable type; technical scope including field of application; schedule of tasks; supporting Full/Associate member identities; the Rapporteur; and, if any, environmental aspects, **user and consumer aspects**, **labour and workplace aspects**, **specific relevance to SMEs**, **security and privacy aspects**, and the WI's position in a hierarchical tree.
  - **Support threshold**: a WI shall be supported by **at least four (4) Full and/or Associate members**, recorded in the creation proposal. A WISR **should** have the same four-member support and must record the **SReq reference**; for a mode-5 Joint Technical Body the **leading ESO is indicated for each WISR**.
  - **Adoption**: the Technical Groups adopt the WIs they create.
- **Progress, milestones and stopping (TWP 1.6.4–1.6.5)**:
  - A TG **shall not approve the final draft** of a deliverable until the corresponding Work Item **has been adopted**.
  - The TG **shall regularly review its Work Items** and keep the ETSI Work Programme updated; changed WIs remain flagged **"changed"** until the end of the month following the change; if a WI has direct relevance to users, the Chair **informs the ETSI User Group**.
  - **Two milestone families**: **decision milestones** — Work Item adoption, Working Group endorsement, Technical Group endorsement, and adoption by the TG / ETSI membership / NSOG / NSBG (set and updated by the TG or WG); and **maturity milestones** — Early draft, Stable draft, Mature draft (**informal, for SReq deliverables**), Final draft (updated by the **Rapporteur**). The maturity ladder carries an explicit warning: *Early draft* should discourage any implementation attempt while *Stable draft* signals no major technical changes expected.
  - **Change control** (Annex L) may be adopted by TGs/WGs, with changes made by **change request** (Annex M) — except comments received during Public Enquiry.
  - **Stopping a WI**: a WI **may** be stopped by TG decision during drafting and before publication if no longer required; a WI **shall** be stopped if drafting has not progressed for **one (1) year**. It is flagged "Stopped" until the end of the following month. (WISRs have their own rule, tied to the SReq.)
  - **PAS Work Items** (Publicly Available Specifications) produce **TS or TR only**, with the **Rapporteur designated by the PAS Submitter** (Annex Q form).
- **Decision-making in a Technical Group (TWP 1.7)** — Consensus first, then a distinctive vote:
  - In **all** decisions, including the appointment of Chairs and Vice-Chairs, the TG **shall endeavour to reach Consensus**. If Consensus cannot be achieved (in a meeting or between meetings), the Chair may hold a vote — a **secret ballot** for Chair/Vice-Chair appointments (per RoP 11.6) and a secret ballot **if any member requests it**.
  - The Chair is responsible for the voting procedure and for maintaining confidentiality in a secret ballot. **Abstentions and failures to vote are not considered** in determining the result.
  - Where a vote is held, **Weighted Individual Voting per RoP Art. 11.2.3 applies**; a Chair appointment follows the RoP Art. 11.4 election procedure.
  - **The two-stage threshold**: a proposal is approved if **71 % of the votes cast** are in favour; **if 71 % is not achieved, the result is re-calculated using the votes of Full members only** — and if that reaches 71 %, the proposal is **deemed approved**. This recalculated fallback is unique to technical-group decisions.
  - **Voting in a meeting**: the Chair states a clear definition of the proposal(s) or candidate list before opening; only registered and present (physically or remotely) delegates of Full and Associate members may vote; **one vote per member organization** and **one per Academic/Corporate/Public Group**; electronic votes may be changed until close; Counsellors' opinions should be noted; **voting by proxy is not permitted**; **there are no quorum requirements**; **splitting of the vote is not permitted**; the Chair evaluates and communicates the result, which is recorded as a decision in the meeting report.
  - **Voting by correspondence**: the proposal is disseminated to the membership list **at least seven calendar days** ahead; only subscribed delegates vote; electronic voting with a voting period of **at least two calendar days**; same one-vote-per-member/Group, no-proxy, no-quorum, no-splitting rules; the result is disseminated **within seven calendar days** and recorded as a correspondence decision in the next meeting's report.
  - **Consensus between meetings**: the proposal and a comment/objection period are disseminated; only subscribed delegates participate; the **Remote Consensus tool** is used; the period is **at least seven calendar days**; objections may be cleared before closing; the **Chair ratifies** the decision at the end based on comments and objections, disseminates within seven days, and records it as a consensus decision.
  - **Appeal**: any Full or Associate member questioning the Chair's ruling may escalate to the **Board** under RoP Art. 22.2 — and **the Chair's ruling stands as the basis for future operations unless overturned by the Board**.
- **The approval architecture (TWP 2.2)** — this is the heart of the instrument:
  - **EN — European Standard (RoP Art. 13)**: adopted via **ENAP**, voted by eligible **NSOG** members using Weighted National Voting, after **Standstill** and a **Public Enquiry**; adoption requires the NSOG vote conditions in RoP Art. 13.
    - **ENAP** and **SRdAP** comment and resolution reporting uses the template in **Annex J**, and the SRdAP flowcharts are **Annex U** (informative).
  - **ES / EG — ETSI Standard / ETSI Guide**: adopted via the **Membership Approval Process (MAP)** under RoP Art. 14:
    1. the TB approves the draft and submits it to the Secretariat **within 14 calendar days**;
    2. if the draft complies with the **ETSI Drafting Rules**, the Secretariat prepares it for member submission **within 30 calendar days**;
    3. the **vote runs 60 calendar days** (extendable for a justifiable reason), each Full and Associate member voting **unconditionally YES / NO / abstention**;
    4. **a "NO" must be accompanied by comments** explaining why the draft is unacceptable;
    5. the result follows RoP Arts. 11.3 and 14 — **abstentions and failures to vote do not count**;
    6. a voting report is made available automatically to the TB and members; if the vote **fails**, the TB Chair decides how to proceed with the Work Item and the NO comments go to the Chair; if it **succeeds**, the Secretariat **publishes within 10 calendar days without modifications other than editorial**, and the NO comments are stored for eventual later revision.
    - **Maintenance**: non-editorial changes require **MAP again**; editorial-only changes are published by the Secretariat on the **TB Chair's confirmation**.
  - **TS / TR — Technical Specification / Technical Report**: adopted by the **TB itself** under Clause 1.7 and submitted to the Secretariat within 14 calendar days — **no membership vote**. Withdrawal is by the TB responsible for maintenance; for a TS the Secretariat first checks whether it is **normatively referenced** in another ETSI deliverable; withdrawal may cover one, several or all versions; for a **terminated TB** whose deliverables are unassigned, the DG consults the membership and puts the comments to the **Board for decision**.
  - **SR — Special Report**: may be produced by a TB (non-technical information), by an **ad-hoc group** of the GA, Board or OCG, or by the **Secretariat/DG** on their behalf; on compliance with the Drafting Rules the Secretariat publishes it.
  - **GS / GR — Group Specification / Group Report**: produced by **ISGs**, which are responsible for validating GR/GS only.
  - **"Historical" status**: documents marked Historical are **kept unchanged in the ETSI Work Programme** until a decision is taken to withdraw them.
- **ISGs (TWP 3)** — the fast, industry-driven lane:
  - An ISG exists **alongside** the Technical Organization, organized around Work Items addressing a specific technology area, **established on the initiative of at least four (4) Full and/or Associate members (or applicants) requesting the Director-General** (Annex D.3 criteria).
  - It defines, creates and stops its own detailed Work Items; validates **GR/GS only**; has its own programme management and delivery dates; reports to the GA and Board on request.
  - **Two explicit exclusions**: an ISG **shall not deal with activities mandated by EC/EFTA**, and **should not** deal with health, safety, environment, public interest, or the basic legal/economic interests of consumer-related issues.
  - Lifecycle covers creation, extension, **transfer of activity to a TC if needed**, and cessation.
- **Coordination Groups (TWP 4–5)**: Clause 4 covers **CGs initiated by ETSI** (characteristics, creation/cessation, chairs, participation, meetings, work programme, decision-making, reporting, Secretariat support); Clause 5 covers **CGs initiated by external bodies** (ETSI's participation, appointment of ETSI representatives and candidates to official positions, decision-making, Secretariat support). The OCG is **not** a Coordination Group (see its ToR).
- **Public working documents (TWP 6)**: conditions for making TC/EP/ISG **draft deliverables**, **individual contributions**, and **any other documents** public from open Docbox areas.
- **SDGs (TWP 7)** — the software lane:
  - An SDG is a **Software or open-source software development activity hosted by ETSI**, proposed by **at least four (4) Full and/or Associate members (or applicants)** requesting the DG.
  - **Agreements**: an **SDG Member Agreement including a Contributor License Agreement** signed by all members wishing to participate; optionally an **SDG Participant Agreement** with CLA for non-members, and an **SDG Individual Contributor Agreement** for individuals.
  - Its **ToR define the licence under which software is contributed and the licence(s) under which it is made publicly available**; it defines its own detailed working procedures; **manages its own budget**; and is responsible for the software and documentation it produces.
  - **It shall not develop ETSI Deliverables as defined in TWP Annex A** — the sharpest boundary in the instrument.
- **Annexes (A–U)** — note the taxonomy, because normative versus informative changes the weight of what you read:
  - **Normative**: A (Definitions), B, C (handling of deliverables from the previous regime), D (requirements for establishing a new body), E (choice of deliverable type), J (template for ENAP/SRdAP comments and resolution reports), L (use of the Change Request process), N, P (the Basic Co-operation Agreement, with four annexes: Joint Presidents' Group ToR; the **5 modes of co-operation**; ESOs' rights and responsibilities in the different modes; document history).
  - **Informative**: H (guidelines for STF/TTF implementation), K (WI/PAS proposal forms), M (change requests), Q (PAS Work Item form), R (overview of ETSI partnership engagements), S, T, U (SRdAP flowcharts).
  - **Void**: F, I, O.
  - **The 5 modes of co-operation** with CEN/CENELEC are: **Mode 1** Informative relation; **Mode 2** Contributive relation; **Mode 3** Sub-contracting relation; **Mode 4** Collaborative relation; **Mode 5** Integrated relation (the basis for a Joint Technical Body and its leading-ESO rule).

## Key Concepts

- **Technical Organization**: EPs, TCs and EPPs collectively, plus the wider working structures.
- **Rapporteur**: one named individual per Work Item, the prime technical contact and owner of maturity milestones.
- **Decision vs maturity milestones**: formal gates versus the Rapporteur's status indications.
- **WI vs WISR**: member-proposed versus SReq-derived Work Items.
- **MAP**: the 60-day membership vote for ES/EG.
- **ENAP / SRdAP**: the EN approval processes for ETSI-originated and SReq-derived European standards.
- **Mode 5 Integrated relation**: the deepest CEN/CENELEC cooperation mode, producing Joint Technical Bodies.
- **ISG exclusion**: no EC/EFTA-mandated activities.
- **SDG exclusion**: no ETSI Deliverables — software and documentation only.
- **"Historical"**: retained-but-frozen status pending a withdrawal decision.
- **Remote Consensus tool**: the between-meetings consensus mechanism.

## Mental Models

- "**Pick the body type by what you are building**" — a market sector with an end date (EP/EPP), a technology area without one (TC), external partners (EPP), a fast industry lane (ISG), or software (SDG).
- "**The Work Item is the atom**" — one responsible group, one Rapporteur, one deliverable per WI; nothing gets approved before the WI is adopted.
- "**Consensus, then 71 %, then 71 % of Full members**" — the two-stage threshold is the technical-group vote's distinguishing feature.
- "**Two speeds of approval**" — ES/EG need all members (MAP, 60 days); TS/TR are the TB's own decision.
- "**No quorum, no proxy, no splitting**" — technical-group votes are member-weighted but procedurally light.
- "**Annexes have jurisdictions**" — normative annexes bind; informative annexes guide; void annexes preserve numbering.
- "**A ceasing body tidies up before it goes**" — publish or stop the WIs, and find a maintainer for the deliverables.

## Anti-patterns

- **Approving a final draft before the Work Item is adopted**: expressly prohibited.
- **Letting a Work Item sit: a year of no progress forces a stop** — the "shall be stopped" rule is automatic, not discretionary.
- **Proxy-voting or splitting votes in a Technical Group**: neither is permitted, and there is no quorum requirement to fall back on.
- **Assuming a failed 71 % vote ends the matter**: the result is recalculated on Full members' votes alone and may still be approved.
- **Treating the Chair's ruling as suspended pending appeal**: it stands as the basis for future operations unless the Board overturns it.
- **Expecting a TS or TR to be membership-voted**: only ES/EG (MAP) and ENs (ENAP/SRdAP) go to the wider membership or NSOG/NSBG.
- **Asking an ISG to handle an EC Standardisation Request**: expressly excluded — that work belongs to the SRdAP track.
- **Expecting an SDG to produce an ETSI deliverable**: it must not; it produces software and documentation under its own licence terms.
- **Overlooking user relevance**: the Chair must inform the ETSI User Group when a WI has direct user relevance, and WI proposals carry user/consumer, SME, security/privacy, environmental and labour aspects.

## Worked Example

**From proposal to published ES.** Trace the full chain:

1. **Work Item**: at least **four Full and/or Associate members** support the creation proposal; the WG/TG records the parameters (title, deliverable type, scope, schedule, supporters, Rapporteur, and the user/SME/security/environmental aspects); a **single Rapporteur** is named; the **TG adopts** the WI. If the subject does not fit one TG, it goes to the **OCG** for allocation.
2. **Drafting**: progress is tracked through **maturity milestones** (early → stable → mature → final) updated by the Rapporteur, and **decision milestones** (WG endorsement, TG endorsement) set by the group.
3. **TG approval**: the TG approves the final draft under Clause 1.7 — **Consensus first**, else a weighted vote needing **71 % of votes cast**, with the **recalculation on Full members' votes** if that fails; no quorum, no proxy, no splitting.
4. **MAP**: the TG submits to the Secretariat **within 14 days**; the Secretariat prepares for member submission **within 30 days** on Drafting-Rules compliance; the **membership vote runs 60 days** with unconditional YES/NO/abstention and **mandatory comments on NO**; abstentions and non-votes are excluded.
5. **Outcome**: on success the Secretariat **publishes within 10 days without modifications other than editorial**, and the NO comments are stored for later revision; on failure the **TB Chair decides** how to proceed and receives the comments.
6. **Maintenance**: later non-editorial changes repeat the MAP; editorial-only changes are published on the **TB Chair's confirmation**.

- Takeaway: the timeline is fully specified — 14 + 30 + 60 + 10 days plus drafting — and the only step a member can block is the 60-day vote, which requires comments to be useful.

## Key Takeaways

1. Three Technical Body types with distinct bases: EP (market sector, limited), TC (technical area, unlimited), EPP (external partners, own rules and legal status).
2. A Work Item is the atom of work: one responsible group, one Rapporteur, one deliverable per WI, four-member support, adoption before any final-draft approval, and **automatic stop after a year of no progress**.
3. Technical-group decisions are Consensus-first with a two-stage threshold — **71 % of votes cast, then 71 % of Full members' votes** — and no quorum, proxy or vote splitting.
4. ES/EG go through the **60-day MAP**; TS/TR are adopted by the Technical Body alone; ENs go through **ENAP** (NSOG) or **SRdAP** (NSBG).
5. ISGs may not handle EC/EFTA-mandated work; SDGs may not produce ETSI Deliverables — two hard boundaries.
6. Annexes carry jurisdiction: normative (A–E, J, L, N, P), informative (H, K, M, Q–U), void (F, I, O).
