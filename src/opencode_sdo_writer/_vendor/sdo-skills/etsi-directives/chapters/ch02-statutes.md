# Chapter 2: Statutes

**Version adopted by the Specially Convened Meeting of the General Assembly #82 (28–29 November 2023)** — 19 Articles. The constitutive instrument: it creates the Institute, defines its purpose and organs, and sets the amendment threshold.

## Core Idea

The Statutes are ETSI's constitution: they found the association under French law, enumerate its organs and their competences, and — critically — reserve amendments of themselves and the Rules of Procedure to a Specially Convened General Assembly by qualified majority.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **The Institute's legal foundation (Art. 1)**: an association founded under the French law of 1 July 1901, non-profit, titled **"EUROPEAN TELECOMMUNICATIONS STANDARDS INSTITUTE"** (ETSI).
- **Purpose (Art. 2)**: to produce and maintain the technical standards and other deliverables required by its members; as a **recognized European Standards Organization**, to produce standards necessary for a large unified European market; to contribute to worldwide standardization; may act by any lawful means.
  - When to use: testing whether an activity is *intra vires*.
- **Scope of activities (Art. 3)**: technical pre-standardization and standardization in **ICT at the European level**:
  - telecommunications, ICT, and other electronic communications networks and services;
  - areas common to telecom/ICT/electronic communications and information technology, **in co-ordination with CEN and CENELEC**;
  - areas common to telecom/ICT/electronic communications and **broadcasting** (especially audio-visual and multi-media), in coordination with **CEN, CENELEC and the EBU**.
  - Plus: contribution to worldwide standardization; openness to co-operation with other organizations; building on existing worldwide standards.
- **Membership (Art. 6)**: categories *and* statuses are separate axes.
  - **Categories** (11, reflecting main activity): Administrations; Other Governmental Bodies; National Standards Organizations; Network Operators; Manufacturers; Users; Service Providers; Research Bodies; Universities; Consultancy Companies/Partnerships; Others (if complying with Art. 3).
  - **Statuses** (3): Full member; Associate member; Observer member. "Member(s)" covers all statuses unless the context says otherwise.
  - A member may participate individually and/or grouped in national or European organizations; must demonstrate interest and comply with the Directives and GA decisions.
  - **Full members** must be established in a CEPT-area country (Art. 6.4). **Associate** status is for applicants not fully meeting Full conditions (6.5). **Observer** status is for those fulfilling Full/Associate conditions but choosing not to participate fully (6.6). All members may attend GA meetings (6.7).
- **Admission (Art. 7)**: conditions and contributions governed by the General Assembly. Applications are approved by **Consensus among members via online poll organized four times per year**; failing Consensus, the application goes to the next Ordinary GA meeting.
- **Termination of membership (Art. 8)**: by dissolution, abolition, resignation, or expulsion.
  - Expulsion for **non-payment** → decided by the **Director-General**.
  - Expulsion for **any other substantial breach** → decided by the **General Assembly**, the member having been invited by registered letter to appear before the meeting.
- **Resources (Art. 9)**: contributions from Full and Associate members; fees from Observer members; grants including EU/EFTA funding; revenue from assets; sums for services; any other authorized resources.
- **Structure (Art. 10)** — the Institute comprises: General Assembly; Board; National Standards Organizations Group (NSOG); National Standardisation Bodies Group (NSBG); a **Technical Organization** (Technical Committees, ETSI Projects, ETSI Partnership Projects); Special Committees; Industry Specification Groups; Software Development Groups; Coordination Groups; and a **Secretariat headed by a Director-General**.
- **General Assembly (Art. 11)**: the **highest authority**, constituted by all members. Full and Associate members have the right to vote. The GA makes or ratifies acts concerning the Institute, **adopts the Rules of Procedure**, and its resolutions are **binding on all members**. EC and EFTA-secretariat representatives have special **Counsellor** status with **no right to vote**. Chair and one or more Vice-Chairs are GA-elected.
- **Meetings of the GA (Art. 12)**:
  - **Ordinary** — twice a year; **≥ 30 calendar days' notice** with draft agenda and supporting documents; the DG accounts for management and finances and annually presents accounts and budget for approval.
  - **Extraordinary** — convened by the Chair, or on written request of **at least 20 Full members**; **≥ 15 calendar days' notice**.
  - **Specially Convened** — for changing the Statutes and Rules of Procedure (see Art. 18).
  - Only matters on the agenda may be considered. Voting procedures and quorum are in the Rules of Procedure. Guests from non-member organizations may be invited. Meetings are **hybrid** (physical + remote) by default, but may be held physically or electronically if so decided.
  - Non-ETSI member National Standards Organizations from CEPT countries may attend free of charge, with no vote.
- **The Board (Art. 13)**: acts on behalf of the GA **between GA meetings** by exercising delegated powers. Appointed by the GA. Nominations by Full and Associate members, but **only representatives of Full members can be Board members**. All members have access to Board documents, may submit views, and may **appeal to the GA** against any Board decision. The RoP provides the Board's powers/functions, appointment procedures, size/composition, and rules of operation (voting, quorum).
- **Technical Organization (Art. 14)**: prepares standards and other relevant deliverables; may be given appropriate autonomy via RoP provisions; **all members may appeal to the Board and the GA** against any decision taken at Technical Organization level.
- **Director-General (Art. 15)**: appointed by the GA; **ex-officio non-voting** member of both GA and Board; **legal representative** of the Institute; holds **chief executive authority** in all matters except those expressly reserved for the GA, Board, NSOG, NSBG or Technical Organization; may temporarily delegate to a nominated substitute.
- **Secretariat (Art. 16)**: provides logistical support to and assists the operation of the Institute.
- **Auditor (Art. 17)**: the GA appoints an external auditor to certify yearly accounts and financial statements, and sets the auditor's remuneration.
- **Amendments (Art. 18)**: the Statutes and Rules of Procedure may **only** be amended by decision of the GA in a **Specially Convened Meeting** called with **not less than six (6) weeks' notice**, accompanied by an agenda and all documents containing the proposed amendments. Decisions are taken by **qualified majority** per Rules of Procedure provisions.
- **Dissolution, winding up (Art. 19)**: the dissolving GA simultaneously fixes liquidation terms, appoints one or more liquidators, determines their powers, and assigns remaining assets after liabilities; any balance of liabilities is borne by the members.

## Key Concepts

- **Recognized European Standards Organization**: the status anchoring ETSI's European mandate (Art. 2).
- **CEPT geographical area**: the eligibility area for Full membership.
- **Consensus (admission)**: agreement sought via online poll four times a year before escalation to the GA.
- **Counsellor**: EC/EFTA-secretariat representative — voice, no vote.
- **Qualified majority**: the amendment threshold for Statutes/RoP, with the exact figure in the Rules of Procedure.
- **Binding resolutions**: GA resolutions bind all members, including those that voted against.
- **Ex-officio non-voting**: the Director-General's status on GA and Board.

## Worked Example

**Who may expel a member, and how does the route differ?** Two scenarios:

1. **Non-payment of contribution or fee** → expulsion decided by the **Director-General** (Statutes Art. 8). No GA involvement; the DG's chief executive authority (Art. 15.2) covers it.
2. **Substantial breach of member obligations (other than non-payment)** → decision by the **General Assembly**, and the member must first have been invited **by registered letter** to appear before the meeting (Art. 8).

- Takeaway: the identity of the decision-maker follows the *character* of the breach, and the procedural safeguard (registered-letter invitation) attaches only to the GA route — a member-facing process point that is easy to miss.

## Key Takeaways

1. The Statutes found ETSI under French 1901 law and enumerate its organs; every other instrument derives its authority from here.
2. The GA is the highest authority and its resolutions bind all members; only Full and Associate members vote.
3. Amendment of the Statutes and Rules of Procedure is tightly gated: Specially Convened GA, ≥ 6 weeks' notice, qualified majority.
4. Full members must be CEPT-established; Associate and Observer statuses cover the other cases; admission runs on a four-times-yearly Consensus poll.
5. Appeal rights are general: members may appeal Board decisions to the GA and Technical Organization decisions to both the Board and the GA.
