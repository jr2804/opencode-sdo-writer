# Chapter 7: Board Instruments

Three documents govern the Board: **RoP Annex 7 — Rules of operation for the Board** (normative, carried by the RoP), the **Board Working Procedures** (Board #148, 5–6 June 2024), and the **Powers and Functions delegated to the Board** (GA #87a, 27 August 2026). Read together: Annex 7 says *who the Board is*, the Powers and Functions say *what it may decide*, and the Working Procedures say *how it meets*.

## Core Idea

The Board is a **delegated body acting for the General Assembly between meetings** — so its competence is enumerated (*only* the 35 delegated functions), its members act **in an individual capacity rather than as representatives of their employers**, and its decisions default to **50 % of votes cast** with a **21-day correspondence window**, both far lighter than the GA's weighted 71 % machinery.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Composition (Annex 7 §1)**: the Board comprises **elected** and **ex-officio non-voting** members.
  - **Ex-officio non-voting**: the Chair of the General Assembly, the Chair of the Finance Committee, the Chair of the NSB Group, the Chair of the NSO Group, the **3SI Advocate**, and the **Director-General**.
  - **Elected members may not send replacements.**
  - **Counsellors** may participate in Board meetings without the right to vote; the Board Chair may invite **Guests**, also without a vote.
- **Election of Board members (Annex 7 §2)**:
  - The DG invites Full and Associate members to submit nominations **at least two months** before an election; candidates may declare an interest in the Board Chair role at any time before the election; the **candidate list is announced 30 calendar days** before the election.
  - Each candidate must be a **representative of a Full member for the mandate period**, supported by a letter from that member's Official Contact; **a member (or an Academic/Corporate/Public Group) may support only one candidate**.
  - The GA **may allocate reserved seats** to ensure representation of specific membership categories or types (e.g. Users, SMEs).
  - **Weighted Individual Voting applies**, and here comes the notable exception: each Full or Associate member (or the single member representing a Group) **may use as many votes as its individual weight and may split them**. (This is the specific carve-out Annex 7 makes from the RoP's general rule that a weighted vote is indivisible.)
  - The GA defines the number of seats to be filled, **between 15 and 30**, elected by highest votes cast; unfilled reserved seats are filled from remaining candidates under GA rules.
  - **Mandate period: three (3) years.** The GA defines the rules for **interim elections** when vacancies arise.
- **Acting individually (Annex 7 §3)**: elected Board members act **in an individual capacity rather than as a direct representative of an ETSI member**, in the interests of the Institute and the membership — and must **inform the Director-General if they are no longer supported** by their supporting member.
- **Self-governance (Annex 7 §4)**: within the framework of the Statutes and Rules of Procedure, **the Board may develop its own working rules and procedures** — which is exactly what the Board Working Procedures instrument is.
- **Dismissal (Annex 7 §5)**: Board members may be dismissed by the **General Assembly** if a problem makes them unable to perform their role. The process is **initiated by the Director-General at the request of at least 20 eligible voters**, and requires a **Weighted Individual Vote** under RoP Art. 11.2.3.
- **Loss of supporting member (Annex 7 §6)**: the Board is kept informed of changes. A member who loses Full-member support but wishes to continue gets a **three-month transition** to find new support, during which they may attend and contribute and retain document and mailing-list access, **but without the right to vote**. If support is not found, the **DG assumes the resignation**, the seat is declared vacant, and — if the Board still holds more than the minimum number of members — **no election is held**; a reserved seat triggers an automatic election process. In all cases, a Full member or Group may not end up supporting more than one Board member.
- **Chair of the Board (Annex 7 §7)**: appointed by the **General Assembly** from among elected Board members for the mandate period, within term limits:
  - up to **two consecutive terms**;
  - beyond two consecutive terms only if applied for **after the candidate list is published** and provided no other Board member candidate objects/competes under the stated conditions;
  - after reaching the maximum, a new series of terms only **after a gap of at least one term**;
  - the limits bind **both the person and the member organization** (or the grouping belonging to the same Group).
  - **Loss of support** triggers immediate notification; if the Chair wishes to continue there is a **three-month transition** during which a **Vice-Chair acts as Board Chair**, and the Chair may attend as a **Guest** with document/mailing-list access but may **neither chair meetings nor submit written contributions**. If no new support is secured, the DG assumes resignation and declares the position vacant — with a call for candidates and an election if the resignation occurs no later than six months before the next regular Board election, or election at the next regular election if less than six months remain. If new support **is** secured, the DG immediately initiates a **confirmation vote of all Full and Associate members**: **≥ 71 % of the weighted individual votes cast** approves the change, whereupon the Chair **immediately resumes**; below 71 %, resignation is assumed.
- **Board officers (Working Procedures §2)**: the Board elects **at least one Vice-Chair** by **secret ballot**, the highest number of votes winning; the Board may assign special functions to individual members where needed; the **Secretariat provides a Secretary**.
- **Meeting cycle and location (Working Procedures §§3–5)**:
  - meeting frequency is adjusted to the tasks; next year's schedule should be available to the **Autumn General Assembly**;
  - the **draft agenda is announced to all ETSI members at least 30 calendar days** before the meeting; **specially convened** Board meetings may be held on the Chair's proposal or on request of a **majority of Board members**;
  - by default meetings are held at **ETSI premises in Sophia Antipolis** (for efficient access to Secretariat resources); they may be held externally **once or twice a year** on invitation of Board members, subject to Board agreement; and on invitation of **Full and Associate members not represented on the Board**, usually within Europe, where one of four stated conditions holds (specific agenda items require it; the Board profits from combination with another relevant event; ETSI obtains a specific benefit such as new members, working arrangements or work areas; or another ETSI promotional activity cannot meet the inviting member's needs). All external invitations are assessed **case by case**.
- **Special attendance (Working Procedures §6)**:
  - Chairs of TCs, EPs, ISGs, Special Committees and SDGs **may be invited** by the Board Chair to a particular meeting, and **may be requested to leave temporarily**; the Chair's intention to invite should be clearly stated;
  - representatives of other standardization organizations or specification providers may attend as **Guests** for particular agenda items;
  - **ETSI members may request Guest attendance**, making the request to the Chair **three weeks** before the meeting. A Guest with **significant commercial or societal interests** in an agenda item **has the right to speak and to present a written contribution**, and may **request deferment** of any related decision;
  - Guests are normally **limited to five (5)**; requests are honoured **first-come, first-served**; previously-attending Guests receive **lower priority**; names are indicated to the Board in advance; Guests may be asked to leave during **confidential or personal** matters;
  - members already represented through a Board member are **not expected** to send Guests.
- **Minutes (Working Procedures §7)**: non-verbatim and of a summary nature, but **all matters discussed and the background to decisions** must be clearly recorded.
- **Documentation (Working Procedures §8)**: numbered per **TWP §1.5.3**; decision-document cut-off normally **seven (7) calendar days** before the meeting; late submissions are marked but **do not prevent a decision**, though any Board member who feels insufficiently informed **may request deferral**; documents are classified **Decision / Discussion / Information**, and decisions may be taken on Discussion/Information documents **provided there are no objections**.
- **Decision-making (Working Procedures §9)**: the Board **shall endeavour to reach Consensus on all issues**. If Consensus cannot be achieved during or between meetings, the Chair may **take a vote**, **defer to a later Board meeting**, or **defer to the General Assembly**.
- **Quorum and voting (Working Procedures §10)**: quorum is **more than half of the elected Board members** present or connected electronically; **without quorum the meeting may proceed but no decision in the name of the Board can be taken**; each elected member has **one vote**; a vote becomes **confidential if one or more members request it**; the default majority is **50 % of the votes cast**, though the Board may vary this in individual cases.
- **Voting by correspondence (Working Procedures §11)**: permitted between meetings when necessary, normally over **21 full calendar days**; the Board Secretary dispatches an announcing email with the documents; the result is announced immediately after closure and the decision is numbered as a decision of the next Board meeting; **lack of quorum (fewer than 50 % of members replying) or a negative result automatically defers the issue to the next meeting**.
- **STF funding decisions — the conflict-of-interest regime (Working Procedures §12)**: the sharpest procedural rules in the Board instruments.
  - STF proposals are presented by the **Chair or a Vice-Chair of the proposing Reference Body** (exceptionally another member of that body, with prior notice to the Board Chair); if no authorized presenter attends, **the proposal is not discussed**.
  - Any Board member with a **potential financial interest — personal or professional, direct or indirect, immediate or future** — in an STF proposal **shall disclose it**. Three named situations qualify: intending personal benefit from the funding; financial dependency on organizations intending to receive it (employee, associate, owner, contractor); or being supported by a Full member seeking benefit from it.
  - A member who has disclosed, and the presenter, may listen to the discussion, access the email exchange, and attend conference calls **without the right to intervene** unless expressly invited — but **shall be requested by the Chair to leave the meeting during the Board's decision-making**.
  - **Failure to comply is itself treated as a conflict of interest**, excluding the member from further participation in that specific STF proposal discussion.
- **The delegated competences (Powers and Functions, 35 items)** — the Board's *only* mandate. Grouped by domain:
  - **Communication and reporting**: communicate relevant information including all Board decisions to all members; provide progress reports to the GA (1–2).
  - **Membership**: consider membership applications where members have intervened in a routine correspondence application and advise the GA as to the category (3).
  - **Policy advice**: advise on broad standardization policies and keep under review the responsiveness, efficiency, timeliness and quality of the standardization arrangements; consider the framework of agreements with external bodies and advise the DG; consider EPP requirements and propose to the GA (4–6).
  - **Technical coordination**: decide on commissioning EPP work from TCs (within prior GA decisions); deal with Standstill problems under RoP Art. 13.3; decide on intermediate stages in draft standards referred by the Technical Organization; resolve RoP disputes and procedural appeals, and Technical Organization disputes, by best endeavours; decide on non-member complaints about deliverables (7–12).
  - **Work Programme and finance**: decide and regularly review the ETSI Work Programme with **user relevance as one of the key parameters**; decide within the GA financial framework on the Funded Work Programme including STF creation and funding; advise the GA on resource implications of Work Programme changes; authorize movement of non-committed funds between budget lines and between years; decide the resource framework for the Technical Organization (13–17).
  - **Organization**: oversee the ETSI organization and propose changes; consider recommendations for new standardization areas, projects and regulatory requirements; decide creation/cessation of TCs and EPs and approve their ToRs; appoint TC/EP Chairs on proposal of the body concerned; propose STF creation/funding **outside** the adopted financial framework; set up Special Committees; decide on approval, maintenance and application of the **Technical Working Procedures** (18–24).
  - **Groups and strategy**: advise the DG on ISG creation/extension/cessation and ToR approval — with the escalation that **where the DG's draft decision departs from the Board's advice, the Board may by Consensus or majority refer the matter to the GA for final decision, notified within 21 calendar days**; prepare the annual **ETSI Strategy** for GA approval and oversee its implementation; oversee annual and multi-annual projects; prepare annual **Budget Guidelines** with the DG and Finance Committee for approval by the year's first GA (25–28).
  - **Partners, coordination and testing**: approve new EPP Partners where a GA-approved Co-operation Agreement exists; decide creation/termination of Coordination Groups and ETSI's participation in externally initiated ones; propose withdrawal of published deliverables whose Technical Body has terminated; review and advise the DG on the technical testing roadmap and **TTF priorities**; promote inclusiveness through specific programmes, advising the GA and DG on participation of **SMEs and societal stakeholders**; advise the DG on SDG creation, extension and cessation (29–35).

## Key Concepts

- **Delegated competence**: the Board acts only within the enumerated Powers and Functions — there is no general residual power.
- **Ex-officio non-voting members**: GA Chair, FC Chair, NSB Group Chair, NSO Group Chair, 3SI Advocate, Director-General.
- **Individual capacity**: Board members act for the Institute, not as instructed delegates of their employers.
- **Split weighted vote**: Annex 7's specific exception to the RoP's indivisible-weighted-vote rule, applying to Board elections.
- **Reserved seats**: GA-allocated Board seats for categories or types such as Users and SMEs.
- **Three-year mandate** with GA-defined interim-election rules.
- **Guest right to speak and deferment**: a Guest with significant commercial or societal interests may speak, submit a written contribution, and request deferral.
- **STF conflict-of-interest disclosure**: mandatory for any financial interest, personal or professional, direct or indirect.
- **Confidence threshold for Board Chair support change**: ≥ 71 % of weighted individual votes cast.

## Mental Models

- "**Three documents, three questions**" — Annex 7 (who), Powers and Functions (what), Working Procedures (how).
- "**The Board is a committee with a closed remit**" — if a function is not in the 35-item list, it is not the Board's to decide.
- "**Individual capacity, collective interest**" — a Board member is not a national or corporate delegate.
- "**Light quorum, light majority, but Consensus first**" — 50 % of votes cast and a 21-day correspondence window, always preceded by a genuine attempt at Consensus.
- "**No quorum, no decisions**" — the meeting can talk, but it cannot decide in the Board's name.
- "**Money decisions have the strictest rules**" — STF funding carries disclosure, exclusion and leaving-the-room obligations found nowhere else in the Board instruments.

## Anti-patterns

- **Treating the Board as having general authority between GA meetings**: its mandate is the enumerated list; the GA retains everything else.
- **Sending a replacement to a Board meeting**: elected members may not send replacements — attendance is personal.
- **Voting as an instructed representative**: Board members act in an individual capacity; the supporting member supplies support, not instructions.
- **Holding a Board decision without quorum**: without more than half of elected members the meeting may proceed but no Board-name decision may be taken.
- **Letting a conflicted member vote on an STF proposal**: disclosure is mandatory; the member must leave during decision-making, and non-compliance is itself a conflict.
- **Assuming a Board election uses an indivisible weighted vote**: Annex 7 expressly permits splitting votes across candidates.
- **Ignoring the 7-day document cut-off**: late documents are allowed but any Board member may force deferral.
- **Allowing an STF proposal to be discussed without an authorized presenter**: it must not be discussed at all.

## Worked Example

**An STF funding proposal with a conflicted Board member.** Company A's representative sits on the Board; Company A intends to bid for the funded work.

1. **Presentation**: the proposal must be presented by the Chair or a Vice-Chair of the proposing Reference Body — if no authorized presenter attends, the item is simply **not discussed** (§12.1–12.2).
2. **Disclosure**: the Board member must disclose the potential financial interest — and being **supported by a Full member that intends to seek benefit** from the funding is one of the three named conflict situations (§12.3–12.4).
3. **Participation limits**: the member may listen, read the email exchange and attend calls, but has **no right to intervene** unless invited, and **must be asked to leave during the decision-making** (§12.5).
4. **Decision**: the Board decides within the GA's financial framework (Powers and Functions 14); for STF creation outside that framework, the Board can only **propose** to the GA (22).
5. **Non-compliance**: failure to follow the above **is itself a conflict of interest**, excluding the member from further participation in that proposal (§12.6).

- Takeaway: only the Board instruments impose a mandatory leave-the-room rule — the funding decisions are where delegated authority is treated as most at risk from self-interest.

## Key Takeaways

1. The Board's competence is enumerated in 35 delegated powers; there is no residual authority between GA meetings.
2. Board members act in an individual capacity, not as representatives of their employers, and cannot send replacements.
3. Annex 7 sets composition (ex-officio non-voting members), 3-year mandates, reserved seats, and the notable **split-vote** election rule.
4. The Working Procedures set a light regime — Consensus first, 50 % of votes cast, 21-day correspondence, 7-day cut-off, no quorum means no decisions.
5. STF funding carries a mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosure and exclusion regime unique among the Board instruments.
6. Loss of a supporting Full member triggers a three-month transition and, for the Board Chair, a ≥ 71 % confirmation vote to resume.
