# Cheatsheet — Decision Guide

## 1. Which instrument governs?

| Question is about… | Instrument | Adopted by |
|---|---|---|
| Founders, organs, membership, amendment of the constitution | **Statutes** | Heads of National Delegations |
| Membership conditions, GA powers, voting, ENs/ES, SReqs, disputes | **Rules of Procedure** | Heads of National Delegations |
| Who votes how, quorums, proxies, escalation | **RoP Arts 11–12, 22** | — |
| Patents/licensing in standards | **IPR Policy (RoP Annex 6)** + Guide on IPR | RoP / Board |
| Competition-law conduct | **Guidelines for Antitrust Compliance** | Board |
| Behaviour in ETSI | **ETSI Values** + Codes of Conduct (members, Board) | General Assembly |
| Board composition, powers, meetings | **RoP Annex 7** + Board Working Procedures + Delegated Powers | RoP / Board / GA |
| Money, budget, contributions' mechanics | **Financial Regulations** (+ RoP Art. 10) | General Assembly |
| FC / GoSR / OCG mandates | **Committee Terms of Reference** | GA / GA / Board |
| Honours and access by former officers | **Courtesy Titles / Fellows / Retired Friends** | Board |
| Technical Bodies, Work Items, approvals | **Technical Working Procedures** | Board |
| Access rights to ETSI documents | **Information Policy** (non-normative) → cite TWP Cl. 6 | Director-General |
| Structure/drafting of deliverables | **ETSI Drafting Rules** | Director-General |

**Rule**: name the instrument and its clause. "The Directives" is not a citation.

## 2. Which voting procedure (RoP 11.2)?

| If the decision is… | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Dissolution; RoP dispute; EN adoption/withdrawal; Statutes/RoP amendment; new National Delegation weighting | **11.2.1 Weighted National Voting** |
| Full-member admonition/rights/termination; EU-regulatory document; EU standardization policy; EU-only Work Programme priority | **11.2.2 Weighted Individual — Full members** |
| **Anything else** | **11.2.3 Weighted Individual — Full + Associate** (default) |

## 3. Thresholds

| Decision | Requires |
|---|---|
| General GA decision | ≥ **71 % of votes cast** (abstentions excluded) |
| RoP amendment (incl. annexes) | ≥ **71 % of total weighted votes** |
| Statutes amendment | ≥ **75 % of total weighted votes**, SCM, ≥ 6 weeks' notice |
| Technical Group decision | ≥ **71 % of votes cast**; else recalculate on **Full members only** |
| Board decision | **50 % of votes cast** (default) |
| Board Chair support-change confirmation | ≥ **71 %** of weighted individual votes cast |
| IPR Policy implementation decision | **71 %** of weighted individual votes |
| Admission to membership | **Consensus** via 4×/year online poll |

## 4. Quorum & mechanics

| Body | Quorum | Proxy |
|---|---|---|
| General Assembly | ≥ 40 % Full-member weighted votes; **+ ≥ 50 %** National-Delegation weight for WNV | allowed, notice ≥ **17 h**, max **3** per decision |
| Board | > half of **elected** members | none stated |
| Technical Group | **none** | **not permitted**; no vote splitting |

## 5. Clocks worth memorising

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Standstill start | **60 days** after WI adoption (or drafting start, later) |
| Standstill derogation decision | ≤ **6 months** |
| NSBG vote on SReq acceptance | **25 days** |
| DG notification of SReq acceptance | **30 days** |
| MAP: submit → prepare → vote → publish | **14 → 30 → 60 → 10 days** |
| GA ordinary notice / extraordinary notice | **30 / 15 days** |
| Board agenda notice / document cut-off | **30 / 7 days** |
| GA decision-document cut-off | **10 days** |
| Technical Group: correspondence vote / remote consensus | ≥ **2 days** / ≥ **7 days** |
| WI stopped if no progress | **1 year** |
| Non-payment → expulsion (DG) | final demand; suspension from **1 June** |
| Contribution paid after **30 April** | **5 % penalty** |

## 6. Deliverable → approval route

| Deliverable | Route |
|---|---|
| EN (ETSI-originated) | **ENAP** — NSOG Weighted National Voting after Standstill + Public Enquiry |
| EN / Harmonised Standard (SReq) | **SRdAP** — NSBG exclusive competence |
| ES / EG | **MAP** — 60-day all-member vote |
| TS / TR | **TB adoption only** — no membership vote |
| GS / GR | ISG validation |
| SR | TB / ad-hoc group / DG on behalf of GA, Board or OCG |

## 7. Technical Body choice

| Need | Use |
|---|---|
| Market sector, defined end, own resources | **EP** |
| Technology area, ongoing, common work | **TC** |
| External partners, own rules/legal status/budget | **EPP** |
| Fast lane, industry-driven, GR/GS only, no EC/EFTA work | **ISG** |
| Software/open source, no ETSI Deliverables | **SDG** |

## 8. Modal verbs (EDR 3.2)

`shall` = requirement · `should` = recommendation · `may` = permission · `can` = possibility · `will` = out-of-scope behaviour · `is` = fact.
**Never** use "must", never "may" for possibility, never "can" for permission, never the present indicative for a requirement.

## 9. Meeting-opening checklist (mandatory)

1. **Antitrust** reminder (with the forbidden-topic list) 2. **Call for IPRs** (declare via ipr.etsi.org) 3. **Code of Conduct** highlights. Then: recognized speakers, no interruption, short interventions, non-native speakers' views, conflicts disclosed.

## 10. Forbidden antitrust topics (in and around meetings)

Pricing; terms of sale/discounts/credit; production levels or capacity; limiting technical development or investment; allocating territories/markets/customers; market shares; bids or intentions to bid; denying market access; refusals to deal; ongoing or threatened litigation. **Even the appearance is prohibited**, and apparent restraints **must be reported**.

## 11. Tells & smells

- Asked to "change the Directives" on the spot → find the **instrument** first; the amendment route may be an SCM with 6 weeks' notice.
- Proposal "passed with 60 %" → check whether abstentions were wrongly counted; the denominator is **votes cast**.
- EN balloted without a Public Enquiry or Standstill → the ENAP preconditions were skipped.
- A draft TS sent for membership vote → **wrong**: TS/TR are TB-adopted.
- An ISG asked to handle an EC SReq → **wrong lane**: that is SRdAP/NSBG work.
- An SDG expected to publish an ETSI Deliverable → **barred**.
- A Technical Group vote that failed 71 % → **recheck on Full members' votes alone**; it may still pass.
- Chair's ruling appealed and treated as suspended → **wrong**: it stands unless the Board overturns it.
- Licensing rates raised in a TB meeting → **antitrust red line**; the only lawful channel is voluntary unilateral public ex-ante disclosure.
- "The Guide says…" used as authority → the **Policy prevails**; Guides and the Information Policy are non-normative.
- Board member voting on an STF bid by their own company → **conflict**: disclose and leave the room.
- Someone citing the IPR Policy for a **commercial** test → essentiality is **technical, not commercial**.
