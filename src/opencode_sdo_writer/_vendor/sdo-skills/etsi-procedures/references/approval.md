# ETSI Approval Routes and Clocks

Once a deliverable is technically endorsed, it follows an approval route.
Which route applies is decided by the deliverable type, not by the group.

## The routes

| Deliverable | Route | Who votes | Clocks |
|-------------|-------|-----------|--------|
| EN (ETSI-originated) | **ENAP** | NSOG, Weighted National Voting, after Standstill and Public Enquiry | Standstill from +60 days; enquiry per the Rules of Procedure |
| EN (SReq-derived) | **SRdAP** | NSBG (competence) | 25-day NSBG vote; 30-day DG notification |
| ES / EG | **MAP** | all Full and Associate members | submit +14 d; prepare +30 d; vote 60 d; publish +10 d |
| TS / TR | **Technical Body adoption** | the technical group, no membership vote | submit to the Secretariat +14 d |
| SR | TB, ad-hoc group or DG on behalf of the GA, Board or OCG | as constituted | published on Drafting-Rules compliance |
| GS / GR | ISG validation | per the ISG ToR | per the ISG ToR |

Two boundaries are hard: an **ISG may not handle an EC/EFTA-mandated
activity**, and an **SDG may not produce an ETSI Deliverable**.

## The technical group vote

| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| Method | Consensus first |
| Threshold | 71 % of votes cast |
| Fallback | recalculate on Full members' votes only |
| Abstentions | not counted |
| Quorum | none |
| Proxy | not permitted |
| Splitting of the vote | not permitted |
| Correspondence voting period | >= 2 calendar days (proposal circulated >= 7 days ahead) |
| Remote consensus period | >= 7 calendar days |

A failed 71 % vote does **not** end the matter: the result is recalculated on
the Full members' votes alone and may still be approved.

## The MAP route, step by step

The 60-day membership vote for an ES or EG is fully specified:

1. the technical group submits to the Secretariat **within 14 days**;
2. the Secretariat prepares for member submission **within 30 days**, on
   Drafting-Rules compliance;
3. the **membership vote runs 60 days** with unconditional YES / NO /
   abstention and **mandatory comments on NO**; abstentions and non-votes are
   excluded;
4. on success the Secretariat **publishes within 10 days**, without
   modifications other than editorial, and stores the NO comments for later
   revision;
5. on failure the **TB Chair decides** how to proceed and receives the
   comments.

The only step a member can block is the 60-day vote, and only with useful
comments.

## Maintenance

- Later **non-editorial** changes repeat the same route (for an ES/EG, a new
  MAP).
- **Editorial-only** changes are published on the **TB Chair's confirmation**.

## Public Enquiry and Standstill

For the ENAP route the National Standardisation Body organises the Public
Enquiry, and the **Standstill** period runs from the +60-day point. A
withdrawal of an EN goes through Weighted National Voting.
